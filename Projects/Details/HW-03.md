## What the Student Does

Copy the published PYNQ-Z2 notebook and its matching bit/hwh to the board. Run FIR filtering on the original sample data, then introduce at least two different noisy signals and compare FPGA results with SciPy filtering. Record the graphs and execution time, explaining coefficient and data-format restrictions.

## Expected Demonstration

1. Run the **original reference project** on the stated board, using its actual precompiled overlay.
2. Explain the original notebook cell by cell and the existing PS–PL interface.
3. Reproduce the author's result and show evidence of real FPGA execution.
4. Change one meaningful input, dataset, parameter, visualization or notebook-level feature, where supported.
5. Document what was reproduced unchanged and what was added, together with results and limitations.

**No FPGA design or generation is required.** Public project/source links are not proof of board compatibility. If the required binary, notebook or peripherals cannot be confirmed, this proposal remains unassigned rather than asking the student to invent hardware.

## Student Submission

Documented adapted `.ipynb`, attribution to the original author, files/dependencies needed to run, screenshots or output plots, explanation of the result and a brief note about modifications.
